1. Color & Filters

A Color Filter only transmits its own color and absorbs all others. In blue light, a red object appears Black because there is no red light to reflect.

2. Refraction & Reflection

Refraction: Light changes speed/direction in different densities. Violet bends most; Red bends least.

Reflection: The angle of incidence always equals the angle of reflection ($i = r$).

3. Wave Anatomy

  • Amplitude: Height (Loudness/Energy).
  • Frequency: Number of waves per second (Pitch).
  • Transverse: Vibrations at 90° to travel (Light).
  • Longitudinal: Vibrations parallel to travel (Sound).

4. The Solar System

  • Day/Night: Caused by Earth's 24-hour Rotation on its axis.
  • Years: Caused by Earth's 365.25-day Orbit around the Sun.
  • Seasons: Caused by the 23.5° Tilt of Earth's axis.
