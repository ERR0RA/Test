Section 1: Botany & Plants

Plants are autotrophs. They create food via Photosynthesis: 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2

ComponentFunction
XylemDead tubes that carry water upwards only.
PhloemLiving tissue that carries sugars in both directions.
StomataPores on the underside of leaves for gas exchange.
Guard CellsControl the opening and closing of stomata.

Section 2: Health & Pathogens

  • Salmonella: Bacteria. Symptoms: Fever, cramps, vomiting.
  • Covid-19: Virus. Spreads via droplets.
  • Ringworm: Fungi. Causes red itchy skin.
  • Ebola: Virus. Extremely high mortality rate.
